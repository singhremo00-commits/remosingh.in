The Musical Origin

I started creating music on my own in 2018. It has always been something I was naturally drawn to, becoming a primary way for me to express thoughts, real feelings, and experiences.

I am completely self-taught with no formal classroom training. Everything I create comes from practicing, experimenting, listening, and learning hands-on—from composing melodies and writing lyrics to recording vocals and mixing tracks.

Writing & Pure Expression

Writing is the most personal part of music for me. I write my own lyrics and compose my melodies, often taking inspiration from simple daily moments, memories, and emotions that linger.

Rather than chasing specific genre trends, I focus on creating music that feels raw, honest, and grounded.

The Break & 2026 Return

From 2021 to 2025, I took a break from active music creation to focus on education and personal responsibilities. Still, the connection to music never stopped.

In 2026, I returned with full creative independence. I am not rushing releases—my focus is simply giving every original track the dedication, sound design, and depth it deserves.
