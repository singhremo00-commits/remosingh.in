About Remo Singh

The journey, the sound, and the stories behind the music.

Remo Singh is a singer and songwriter who has been creating music independently since 2018. Born on 10 March 1994, he has had an interest in music from an early age.

Remo’s musical journey has been entirely self-taught. He has never taken formal training in singing or music. Instead, he learned by himself, experimenting, practicing, and developing his own style along the way.

Since starting his singing journey in 2018, he has created 34 songs, with the lyrics of every song written by him. Writing is an important part of his creative process, and he enjoys expressing his thoughts and emotions through his own words and melodies.

His musical taste leans mostly toward soft, emotional and soulful songs—music that focuses on feelings, stories and simple emotions rather than being limited to a particular style.

For Remo, music is not something he simply learned; it is something he continues to discover, create and learn on his own.
