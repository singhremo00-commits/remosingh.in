Biography

About Remo Singh

Remo Singh is an independent singer and songwriter born on 10 March 1994. Having nurtured an early fascination with melodies, he officially embarked on his singing journey in 2018.

He has not taken formal singing or music training. Instead, Remo Singh is completely self-taught, sharpening his musical sensibilities through dedicated self-learning, regular practice, and creative exploration.

Since starting his musical journey in 2018, Remo Singh has created 34 original songs. He takes profound joy in the craft of songwriting and prefers writing his own lyrics, ensuring that every melody carries an authentic, personal touch.

His musical preference centers primarily on soft, emotional, and soulful compositions designed to connect deeply with listeners.
