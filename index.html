About the Artist

Remo Singh is an independent singer, songwriter, and audio producer. His music blends acoustic guitar fingerpicking, subtle ukulele textures, warm vocals, and indie arrangements.

With a focus on original songwriting, sound engineering, and heartfelt composition, Remo's work brings together acoustic warmth and modern independent production.
