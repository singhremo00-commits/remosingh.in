Music has always been my truest form of expression. Since 2018, I started creating, composing, and producing my own songs completely self-taught. 

Every melody begins with an emotion, an acoustic strum, or a simple thought that stays in my head until it finds its voice.

## Returning to Music in 2026

After taking a personal break to focus on life and studies, stepping back into the studio feels revitalizing. Creating without rush, focusing purely on honest storytelling, and blending modern acoustic vibes with regional roots—especially contemporary Bishnupriya Manipuri folk and indie pop.

I am excited to share new tracks, acoustic arrangements, and behind-the-scenes stories right here. 

Stay tuned for upcoming releases, lyrics breakdowns, and studio moments. Thank you for listening and being a part of this journey!
